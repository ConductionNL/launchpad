# Flowable Case Management Documentation

Source: https://www.flowable.com/solutions/case-management

## Definition

Case management is a strategic approach to managing and coordinating complex processes in ever-changing business environments. It provides the framework for end-to-end automation while keeping humans in the loop, combining automation, orchestration, and human decision-making.

Per Gartner: "Applications designed to support complex processes that require a mix of human tasks and electronic workflows, such as loan applications, claims, or complaints."

## Case Management vs CMMN

| Aspect | Case Management | CMMN |
|--------|----------------|------|
| Scope | Broad concept across many fields | Specific notation for modeling |
| Standardization | No formal standard, flexible | Formal OMG standard, structured |
| Usage | Healthcare, law, customer success | Business process and IT management |
| Flexibility | Highly flexible, human decisions | Framework to model flexibility |
| Tools | May or may not use structured tools | Requires CMMN-supporting tools |
| Decision Making | Human-driven, ad-hoc | Modeled with discretionary tasks |
| Graphical Notation | No specific standard | Graphical elements for cases |

## Benefits of Case Management

1. **Manage complex workflows**: Simplifies unstructured and complex processes
2. **People-centric approach**: Focuses on people and their data/documents
3. **Agility**: Adapt to market shifts, customer events, internal updates
4. **Real-time adaptation**: Instantly respond to new data and conditions
5. **Contextual awareness**: Centralized context for informed decisions
6. **Holistic integration**: Connects systems, services, and vendors
