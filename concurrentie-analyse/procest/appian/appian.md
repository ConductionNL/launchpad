# Appian — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://appian.com/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** Yes (on-prem)
- **Summary:** Low-code case management for public sector

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS and on-premise licensing. Appian is a publicly traded company (NASDAQ: APPN) with revenue from subscription licenses (SaaS and on-premise), professional services, and support contracts. Named a leader in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Enterprise Low-Code Application Platforms. Follows a per-user, per-app, per-month pricing model targeting large enterprise and government customers.

## Target Market

Large enterprises and government agencies globally. Strong presence in US federal government — used by the Department of Defense, every branch of the US Armed Forces, and numerous federal agencies. Also targets financial services, healthcare, insurance, and manufacturing. Focuses on organizations needing process automation, case management, and digital transformation at enterprise scale.

## Pricing

- **Standard:** Per-user/per-app/per-month — includes low-code development, data fabric, integrations, AI Copilot for developers, basic RPA (up to 5 bots), and 1 portal per app
- **Advanced:** Higher per-user cost — adds Process HQ, Case Management Studio, offline mobile, RPA (up to 25 bots), unlimited portals, multiple data sources, AI Copilot for business users
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing with full capabilities
- Minimum user counts and exact pricing require contacting sales

## Key Features

- Low-code application development platform with drag-and-drop interface
- Dynamic case management with Case Management Studio
- Process HQ for enterprise process intelligence
- Data fabric for unified data access across systems
- AI Copilot for both developers and business users
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA) built into the platform
- Offline mobile capabilities for field workers
- Pre-built government solutions (procurement, grants, case management)
- Document processing with AI/ML extraction
- Integration framework with hundreds of pre-built connectors
- FedRAMP authorized for US government cloud deployment

## Strengths

- Enterprise-grade low-code platform with AI, RPA, and process mining in a single offering
- Strong government track record with FedRAMP authorization and defense/intelligence agency adoption
- Massive R&D investment as a publicly traded company — continuous feature development

## Weaknesses

- Very expensive — enterprise pricing puts it out of reach for most Dutch municipalities
- No Dutch government domain knowledge — no ZGW APIs, no Common Ground, no zaakgericht werken
- Proprietary lock-in — no source code access, no self-modification, complete vendor dependency

## Notes

Appian is a global enterprise leader but operates in a completely different segment than Dutch municipal case management. Its pricing alone disqualifies it for most Dutch municipalities. However, it could appear in larger national government (Rijksoverheid) procurement processes. Procest differentiates with open source, Dutch government standards compliance, and a price point accessible to municipalities of all sizes.
