# ArkCase FOIA Module

**Source:** arkcase.com/solutions/arkcase-foia/, arkcase.com blog posts, release notes

## Overview

ArkCase FOIA is the flagship product, built with open standards and state-of-the-art technologies offering government agencies a comprehensive FOIA tracking and management solution. It was created in response to customer frustrations with expensive FOIA solutions that were hard to maintain.

ArkCase FOIA met DOJ INTERPOL's needs by providing a single product for request processing including document review, redaction, and approval workflow.

## Key Customers
- Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC)
- Department of Justice (DOJ INTERPOL)
- University of Washington
- Various federal and state agencies

## FOIA Capabilities

### Automate Case Worker Actions
- Microsoft Office integration
- Activity monitoring per user and/or request
- Correspondence and template management (merge fields)
- Duplicate identification (request and document level)
- Integrated Robotics Process Automation (RPA)
- OCR for scanned content capture and extraction
- Alerts and notifications
- Email ingestion (create requests from incoming mail)

### Improve Requester/Citizen Experience
- **Public Portal** with self-services
  - Submit requests from any source (web, phone, email, fax, walk-ins)
  - Reading Room for existing public records
  - Submit, check status, and cancel requests
  - Download responsive records
  - ZIP download of multiple documents
  - Authenticated and unauthenticated portal modes
  - Configurable anonymous request submissions

### Manage Processes
- Dynamic workflows for requests and appeals
- Configurable queue workflow (Intake, Fulfill, Review, Approve, Hold, Billing, Release, General Counsel, etc.)
- Document review and eDiscovery (Relativity integration)
- Integrated document and records management
- Collaboration tools
- Automated payment processing
- E-delivery of responsive documents
- Integrated audio and video transcription
- Referral section for FOIA and PA requests

## FOIA Best Practices (ArkCase Recommendations)

Based on Center for Effective Government guidelines:

1. **Proactive Online Disclosures** -- post information online to reduce requests
2. **Internet-Based Processing** -- online submission, tracking, email communication
3. **Prompt Acknowledgment and Tracking** -- tracking numbers, status updates
4. **Proactive Communication** -- contact requesters before rejecting
5. **Records Preservation** -- Alfresco-powered DoD 5015.2 compliant records management
6. **Streamlined Confidential Business Information Claims** -- objection workflows
7. **Clear Fee Procedures** -- configurable fee and waiver management
8. **Dispute Resolution** -- exemption code tracking, appeal management
