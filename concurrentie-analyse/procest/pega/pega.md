# Pega Platform — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.pega.com/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** Yes (on-prem)
- **Summary:** Enterprise case management and BPM for government

## Business Model

Proprietary licensing with SaaS and on-premise options. Pegasystems (NASDAQ: PEGA) generates revenue from cloud subscriptions, perpetual licenses, maintenance, professional services, and training. Known as one of the most expensive enterprise platforms in the market. Pricing varies based on users, cases, and specific enterprise needs. Targets the largest organizations willing to invest heavily in digital transformation.

## Target Market

Large enterprises and government agencies globally. Strong in government (State of Maine, DEFRA UK, NSW Australia, TxDOT), financial services, insurance, healthcare, and telecommunications. Focuses on organizations with complex, high-volume case management and customer engagement needs. Pega is positioned as a premium enterprise platform.

## Pricing

- Licensing starts in the $100-$500+ per user/month range depending on capabilities
- Cloud and on-premise licensing options available
- Cloud deployment benefits SMEs by reducing setup time
- Enterprise pricing is customized and typically involves six to seven-figure annual contracts
- High total cost of ownership including implementation, training, and ongoing maintenance

## Key Features

- Dynamic case management with AI-powered case routing and prioritization
- Low-code application development with drag-and-drop interface
- AI-suggested lifecycle and data model generation
- Decisioning engine with real-time business rules and analytics
- End-to-end digital process automation (bots, case management, BPM)
- Dynamic task creation and assignment to individual employees
- AI-suggested field values and data collection
- Customer engagement and journey orchestration
- Robotic process automation (RPA) and robotic desktop automation
- Industry-specific solutions for government, healthcare, financial services
- Multi-channel customer interaction (web, mobile, chat, email)

## Strengths

- Most advanced AI-powered case management with predictive analytics and automated decision-making
- Proven at extreme enterprise scale — handles millions of cases for the world's largest organizations
- Deep vertical solutions for government with pre-built templates and frameworks

## Weaknesses

- Extremely expensive — total cost of ownership far exceeds what Dutch municipalities can afford
- Massive implementation complexity — requires specialized Pega consultants (expensive and scarce)
- Proprietary everything — complete vendor lock-in with no open standards (no CMMN, no ZGW)

## Notes

Pega is the most expensive and feature-rich competitor in this analysis but is not realistically competing for Dutch municipal case management. It appears in the comparison as a reference point for what enterprise-grade case management looks like. The only overlap with Procest might be in national government (Rijksoverheid) or large executive agencies (uitvoeringsorganisaties). Procest's position as an affordable, open-source, standards-compliant alternative is maximally differentiated from Pega.
