# Pipelinq — Concurrentie-analyse

## Product Description

Pipelinq is a **lightweight CRM application** built natively on Nextcloud. It manages clients (persons & organizations), tracks leads through visual kanban pipelines, captures service requests, logs contact moments, and syncs with Nextcloud Contacts via CardDAV. Designed for government and small-to-medium organizations, it integrates with Procest for a complete intake-to-case-resolution workflow.

## Comparable Software Categories

- CRM Systems (lightweight / simple)
- Contact Management Tools
- Lead / Pipeline Management
- Government CRM / Klantinteractie Systems
- Customer Relationship Management (small business)
