# Mozard — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.mozard.nl/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** Integrated case management, DMS, customer contact, and workflow

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS model. Mozard was acquired by Atabix in 2024, strengthening Atabix's market position. Revenue comes from SaaS subscriptions, implementation services, process configuration, training, and ongoing support. The platform is offered as an integrated suite with multiple modules that can be combined based on organizational needs.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities, safety regions (veiligheidsregios), and environmental services (omgevingsdiensten). Primary focus on organizations needing an all-in-one solution for case management, document management, and customer contact. Strong in the VTH domain with Omgevingswet compliance.

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. Subscription-based pricing dependent on selected modules, number of users, and organization size. Contact Atabix/Mozard for a custom quote.

## Key Features

- Integrated suite: zaaksysteem, DMS, klantcontactsysteem, intranet, CMS, and forms in one platform
- Object registration system for structured data management
- Document management system with archiving (RMA) capabilities
- Customer contact system (KCS) for citizen interaction tracking
- Low-code process builder for designing and optimizing workflows
- Configurable forms engine for citizen intake
- DSO integration for Omgevingswet compliance
- Integration with national databases (DigiD, BAG, OLO, DSO)
- Data warehouse module for reporting and analytics
- Intranet module for internal communication
- Full case lifecycle management from request through archiving

## Strengths

- Truly integrated suite with DMS, KCS, forms, intranet, and case management in a single platform — minimal integration needed
- Strong in VTH domain with proven Omgevingswet/DSO compliance
- Low-code process configuration accessible to non-technical municipal staff

## Weaknesses

- Proprietary and closed-source — complete vendor lock-in with the Atabix ecosystem
- No standards-based process modeling (no CMMN/BPMN) — workflows are proprietary
- Recently acquired by Atabix — potential disruption during product integration and strategy alignment

## Notes

Mozard's strength is its integrated approach — everything in one suite. The acquisition by Atabix in 2024 creates both opportunities (larger customer base, more resources) and risks (product overlap with Atabix's own zaaksysteem). Procest competes by offering open standards, self-hosting, and the Nextcloud ecosystem as an alternative to Mozard's monolithic approach.
