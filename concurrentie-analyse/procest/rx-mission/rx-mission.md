# Rx.Mission (Visma Roxit) — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://rxmission.nl/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** Case management for VTH on ZGW API / Common Ground

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS model. Visma Roxit (acquired by Visma) delivers Rx.Mission as a fully managed multi-tenant SaaS solution. Revenue comes from SaaS subscriptions, implementation services, migration support (from legacy SquitXO), and ongoing support. As part of the Visma group, Roxit benefits from shared R&D and infrastructure investment. The company has approximately 200 employees.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities, provinces, safety regions (veiligheidsregios), and environmental services (omgevingsdiensten) working in the VTH domain (Vergunningverlening, Toezicht en Handhaving — permits, supervision, and enforcement). Specifically targets organizations implementing the Environmental Law (Omgevingswet) and needing Common Ground-compliant VTH solutions.

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. SaaS subscription pricing based on organization size and usage. Contact Visma Roxit for a custom quote. Migration pricing from legacy SquitXO system may include transition incentives.

## Key Features

- Complete VTH process support for permits, supervision, and enforcement
- Omgevingswet-proof with full DSO (Digitaal Stelsel Omgevingswet) integration
- Common Ground architecture with data accessible via APIs
- Collaboration space for exchanging DSO requests with chain partners
- Location-based information for risk-based supervision
- Mobile app for offline supervision in the field
- Document creation and management modules
- Archiving functionality
- Support for APV, Events, and Special Laws domains alongside Environmental Law
- Case-oriented working with process guidance
- Risk-based scheduling and enforcement prioritization

## Strengths

- Deep specialization in VTH domain with Omgevingswet compliance built in from the ground up
- Common Ground architecture with genuine API-first design and data-at-the-source principles
- Mobile offline capabilities for field inspectors — a real-world need that few competitors address

## Weaknesses

- Narrowly focused on VTH domain — not suitable as a general-purpose case management system
- SaaS-only with no self-hosting option — organizations cannot control their infrastructure
- Proprietary — despite Common Ground compliance, the platform itself is closed-source

## Notes

Rx.Mission is a domain-specific competitor focused on VTH rather than general case management. It overlaps with Procest primarily for organizations handling permit and enforcement cases. Procest's advantage is flexibility across all case types, while Rx.Mission has deeper VTH-specific functionality. Being part of Visma alongside Visma Circle creates potential bundling opportunities that Procest needs to counter with its own ecosystem story.
