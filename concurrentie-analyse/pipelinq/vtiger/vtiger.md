# Vtiger CRM — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.vtiger.com/open-source-crm/
- **Open Source:** Yes (VPL/MPL)
- **Self-Hosted:** Yes
- **Summary:** Mature open-source CRM with 5M+ downloads

## Codebase

- **Repository:** https://github.com/vtiger-crm/vtigercrm (Community Edition)
- **GitLab Mirror:** https://code.vtiger.com/vtiger/vtigercrm

## Business Model

Hybrid open-core model. The Community Edition is free and self-hosted under the Vtiger Public License (based on Mozilla Public License). Revenue comes from the commercial cloud CRM (Vtiger One) with tiered per-user subscriptions, paid extensions from the marketplace (200+ available), and enterprise support. The cloud version includes AI/ML features not available in the open-source edition.

## Target Market

Small to mid-sized businesses looking for a mature, feature-rich CRM. Over 5 million downloads worldwide. Popular with companies migrating from spreadsheets to a structured CRM. The cloud edition targets teams wanting a managed solution with AI capabilities.

## Pricing

- **Community Edition (self-hosted):** Free
- **One Pilot (cloud):** Free for basic features
- **One Growth:** ~$12/user/month
- **One Professional:** ~$30/user/month
- **One Enterprise:** ~$42/user/month
- Volume discounts available as user count increases

## Key Features

- Contact, lead, account, and opportunity management
- Sales pipeline with forecasting and revenue tracking
- Marketing automation and email campaigns
- Customer support with ticketing and SLA management
- Workflow automation triggered on record save or schedule
- Role-based access control
- Reports, dashboards, and analytics
- Phone and email integration
- Customer self-service portal
- REST API and 200+ marketplace extensions
- AI/ML capabilities in cloud edition (Vtiger Calculus)

## Strengths

- Very mature platform with 5M+ downloads — proven stability and large community
- Rich marketplace with 200+ extensions and language packs for extensibility
- Built-in customer support and SLA management — covers sales + support in one platform

## Weaknesses

- Open-source Community Edition lags behind the cloud version in features (no AI, limited updates)
- No Nextcloud integration or Dutch government ecosystem support
- VPL license is more restrictive than GPL/MIT — file-level copyleft limits some customization

## Notes

Vtiger is one of the oldest open-source CRMs, originally forked from SugarCRM. The Community Edition is functional but receives slower updates compared to the commercial cloud offering. The cloud product (Vtiger One) is competitive with Salesforce at a fraction of the cost. For Dutch government use, the lack of Nextcloud and Common Ground integration is a significant gap.
