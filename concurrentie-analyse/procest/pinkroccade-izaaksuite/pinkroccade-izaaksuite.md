# PinkRoccade iZaaksuite — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.pinkroccadelocalgovernment.nl/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** Zaakgericht werken architecture for municipalities

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS licensing. PinkRoccade Local Government is part of Total Specific Solutions (TSS), a Constellation Software subsidiary. Revenue comes from SaaS subscriptions, implementation services, support contracts, and integration fees. The company operates as a specialist software vendor focused exclusively on the Dutch local government market, leveraging a broad portfolio of municipal software products for cross-selling.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities. PinkRoccade Local Government is one of the largest software suppliers to Dutch municipalities with a significant market share. Focus on organizations that need tight integration between case management and domain-specific applications (permits, social domain, civil affairs, etc.).

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. Enterprise pricing based on municipality size, number of users, and selected modules. Includes per-user licensing, implementation costs, and ongoing support fees. Contact PinkRoccade for a custom quote.

## Key Features

- Central case application disclosing information from both the case system and domain applications
- iZaakafhandeling for handling cases, sub-cases, follow-up cases, and related cases
- iKlantcontactsysteem (KCS) for efficient customer contact handling
- Process modeling tools for designing workflows
- Web intake forms for citizen and business self-service
- Personal Internet Page (PIP) for case status display
- Strong connections with domain-specific task applications
- Document management and archiving
- Integration with national registrations (BRP, BAG, etc.)
- Reporting and management dashboards

## Strengths

- Excellent integration with PinkRoccade's domain-specific applications creating a seamless ecosystem
- Large installed base in Dutch municipalities with deep domain expertise
- Rich case handling and customer contact center functionality rated highly by analysts

## Weaknesses

- Complete vendor lock-in within the PinkRoccade/TSS ecosystem — switching costs are very high
- Proprietary and closed-source with slow adoption of open standards like ZGW APIs and Common Ground
- Owned by Constellation Software (known for acquiring and maintaining, not innovating) — innovation pace is limited

## Notes

PinkRoccade is a dominant player in the Dutch municipal software market through its broad product portfolio. The strength of iZaaksuite lies in its integration with other PinkRoccade products rather than in standalone capabilities. The Common Ground movement is a strategic threat to their integrated-suite approach. Procest's open architecture and standards-based approach offer municipalities an exit from vendor lock-in.
