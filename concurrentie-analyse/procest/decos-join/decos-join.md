# Decos JOIN — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://decos.com/en/government/case-oriented-working
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** Comprehensive case management for Dutch municipalities

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS and on-premise licensing. Decos offers JOIN as a subscription-based cloud service or as an on-premise installation. Revenue comes from software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, implementation services, training, and ongoing support contracts. Decos is a well-established Dutch software company that has been in the government market for decades.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities and island governments (Caribbean Netherlands). Over 100 municipalities and island governments use JOIN. Also targets other public sector organizations and some private sector companies needing document and case management.

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. Pricing is typically based on number of users and selected modules. Organizations must contact Decos for a custom quote. Both cloud and on-premise deployment options available with different pricing models.

## Key Features

- Integrated case and document management in a single platform
- Template management for standardized document generation
- Workflow engine for automated process routing
- E-forms for digital intake of citizen requests
- Case Type Management aligned with GEMMA ZTC 2.0 standards
- Agenda management and meetings management
- Formal administrative decision management (besluiten)
- Digital signatures support
- Case types can be imported from i-Navigator
- Customer portal for citizen self-service
- Integration with national registrations (BRP, BAG, etc.)

## Strengths

- Mature, feature-rich platform with 20+ years of development and refinement
- Large installed base (100+ municipalities) providing extensive domain expertise and proven reliability
- All-in-one solution combining case management, DMS, e-forms, and citizen portal

## Weaknesses

- Proprietary and closed-source — municipalities are locked into the Decos ecosystem with limited flexibility
- Legacy architecture that is slow to adopt modern standards like ZGW APIs and Common Ground
- No CMMN or BPMN support — workflow capabilities are proprietary and less flexible

## Notes

Decos JOIN is one of the market leaders in Dutch municipal case management by installed base. However, it represents the "old guard" of proprietary solutions that the Common Ground movement is pushing municipalities away from. Procest's open-source nature and modern architecture position it well against JOIN for municipalities seeking digital sovereignty.
