## Timeline

- **2019:** Initial concept presentations at VNG
- **2023 September:** KISS Phase 1 delivered
- **2023 June:** First go-live at Municipality of Utrecht (50 employees)
- **2024 H1:** Integration with e-Suite for Dimpact municipalities
- **2024 H2:** Municipality of Deventer goes live
- **2025-2026:** Continued development, current version v2.2.1 (March 2026)

## Dimpact Connection

Dimpact is a cooperative of 40+ Dutch municipalities for shared IT services. KISS is part of the "PodiumD" stack within Dimpact, alongside:
- Open Zaak (case management)
- Open Klant (customer/contact registration)
- Open Formulieren (forms)
- Open Notificaties (notifications)
