# Pipedrive — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.pipedrive.com/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (cloud)
- **Summary:** Visual pipeline-first CRM for sales teams

## Business Model

Pure SaaS subscription model with per-seat pricing. Revenue from monthly/annual subscriptions across four tiers, plus paid add-ons (LeadBooster, Web Visitors, Campaigns, Smart Docs, Projects). Pipedrive was founded in 2010, is headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia, and has 100,000+ customers in 179 countries. Acquired by Vista Equity Partners in 2020.

## Target Market

Small to mid-sized sales teams (5-200 users). Specifically designed for salespeople who want a visual, pipeline-centric CRM that is easy to use. Popular with B2B sales teams, agencies, and startups that prioritize deal tracking over marketing automation.

## Pricing

- **Lite:** $14/seat/month (annual) or $24/seat/month (monthly)
- **Growth:** $39/seat/month (annual) or $49/seat/month (monthly)
- **Premium:** $49/seat/month (annual) or $69/seat/month (monthly)
- **Ultimate:** $79/seat/month (annual) or $99/seat/month (monthly)
- 14-day free trial on all plans, no free tier
- Add-ons: LeadBooster ($32.50/mo), Web Visitors ($41/mo), Campaigns ($13.33/mo)

## Key Features

- Visual kanban pipeline with drag-and-drop deal management
- Contact and organization management
- Email integration with two-way sync (Gmail, Outlook)
- Activity scheduling and reminders
- Sales forecasting and revenue reporting
- LeadBooster for lead generation (chatbot, web forms, prospector)
- Workflow automation (trigger-based)
- Customizable fields and pipelines
- Mobile app (iOS and Android)
- 400+ integrations via marketplace
- AI-powered sales assistant

## Strengths

- Best-in-class visual pipeline UX — the gold standard for kanban-based deal management
- Extremely easy to set up and use — designed by salespeople for salespeople
- Large marketplace with 400+ integrations and proven scalability (100k+ companies)

## Weaknesses

- No self-hosting option — all data in Pipedrive's cloud, no data sovereignty
- No open source — fully proprietary, no code access or customization beyond configuration
- No Nextcloud integration or Dutch government ecosystem support; expensive at scale

## Notes

Pipedrive is the direct UX inspiration for many pipeline CRMs including Pipelinq. Its visual pipeline interface set the standard. However, as a proprietary cloud-only product, it cannot meet requirements for data sovereignty, self-hosting, or Nextcloud integration. The per-seat pricing becomes expensive for larger teams. Pipedrive is the benchmark for pipeline UX that Pipelinq should match or exceed.
