# Freshsales — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.freshworks.com/crm/sales/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (cloud)
- **Summary:** CRM with built-in phone/email and AI-powered lead scoring

## Business Model

SaaS subscription model with per-user pricing across four tiers. Part of the Freshworks product family (also includes Freshdesk, Freshservice, Freshmarketer). Freshworks is publicly traded (NASDAQ: FRSH). Revenue from subscriptions, add-ons, and the broader Freshworks suite. Freshsales Suite (CRM + marketing) available as a combined product.

## Target Market

Small to medium-sized B2B sales teams (5-50 users is the sweet spot). Appeals to teams that want built-in phone and email capabilities without third-party integrations. Popular with companies that already use other Freshworks products (Freshdesk, Freshservice).

## Pricing

- **Free:** Up to 3 users with basic contact and deal management
- **Growth:** $29/user/month (annual) or $35/user/month (monthly)
- **Pro:** $59/user/month (annual) or $69/user/month (monthly)
- **Enterprise:** $99/user/month (annual) or $119/user/month (monthly)
- 21-day free trial available

## Key Features

- Contact, account, and deal management
- Visual sales pipeline with kanban board
- Built-in phone (cloud telephony) with call recording
- Email integration with two-way sync (Gmail, Outlook)
- AI lead scoring and deal insights (Freddy AI)
- Workflow automation with triggers and actions
- Territory management
- Sales sequences (automated outreach cadences)
- Reports and analytics dashboards
- 190+ third-party integrations
- Mobile app (iOS and Android)

## Strengths

- Built-in phone system with call recording — no need for separate VoIP integration
- AI-powered lead scoring (Freddy AI) that improves over time — helps prioritize sales efforts
- Part of the Freshworks ecosystem — seamless integration with Freshdesk, Freshservice, and Freshmarketer

## Weaknesses

- Proprietary cloud-only — no self-hosting option, no data sovereignty
- Per-user pricing becomes expensive for larger teams (Enterprise at $99/user/month)
- No Nextcloud integration or open-source option; limited customization beyond configuration

## Notes

Freshsales stands out for its built-in telephony and AI capabilities. The Freddy AI assistant for lead scoring is a genuine differentiator. However, as a proprietary cloud-only product, it cannot meet data sovereignty or self-hosting requirements. The Freshworks ecosystem is a strength for companies that use multiple Freshworks products but creates vendor lock-in. Not suitable for Dutch government use cases.
