# Visma Circle (Djuma) — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.vismacircle.nl/software/zaakgericht-werken
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** Case management with zaaksysteem-central architecture

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS model. Visma Circle is part of the Visma group, a large Scandinavian software conglomerate. Revenue comes from SaaS subscriptions, implementation services, training, and ongoing support. Djuma is delivered exclusively as SaaS, removing infrastructure management from the customer. The Visma backing provides financial stability and R&D investment capacity.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities, semi-government organizations, and educational institutions. Focus on organizations in the (semi-)government, education, and related markets needing digital process and case-oriented working. Municipalities of various sizes use Djuma, including Dronten, Alphen aan den Rijn, Heerlen, Vlissingen, and Meierijstad.

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. SaaS subscription pricing based on municipality size and number of users. Contact Visma Circle for a custom quote. Being part of the Visma group may enable competitive pricing through economies of scale.

## Key Features

- True SaaS case management system with certified cloud security
- Zaakgericht werken (case-oriented working) as core functionality
- Integration with customer contact center (KCC)
- Online forms for citizen intake
- Contract management module
- Document and records management (DMS/RMA)
- Collaboration tools for internal and inter-organizational use
- E-services and citizen self-service portal
- Private cloud section with certified security
- Quick deployment of new functionalities through SaaS updates
- Integration with national registrations

## Strengths

- Backed by Visma group providing financial stability, R&D resources, and brand recognition
- Pure SaaS delivery means fast deployment, automatic updates, and no infrastructure burden for customers
- Broad product portfolio covering case management, DMS, collaboration, and e-services in one suite

## Weaknesses

- SaaS-only with no self-hosting option — municipalities have no control over data sovereignty
- Proprietary and closed-source — no way to inspect, modify, or extend the platform independently
- Part of a large Scandinavian conglomerate — Dutch municipal domain knowledge may be diluted by corporate priorities

## Notes

Visma Circle is a well-established player in the Dutch government software market. The Djuma zaaksysteem competes directly with Procest in the case management space. Visma's acquisition strategy (they also acquired Roxit) is consolidating the Dutch government software market. Procest differentiates through open source, self-hosting capabilities, and native Nextcloud integration.
