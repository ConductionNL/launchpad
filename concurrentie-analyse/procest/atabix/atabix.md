# Atabix — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.atabix.nl/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (SaaS)
- **Summary:** SaaS-based zaakgericht werken with configurable components

## Business Model

Proprietary SaaS model. Revenue comes from SaaS subscriptions for their modular product suite, implementation services, process configuration, training, and ongoing support. Atabix has grown through acquisitions, recently acquiring Mozard and IenPM to strengthen its market position. The company has 150+ employees and over 25 years of experience in the government software market.

## Target Market

Dutch municipalities, safety regions, environmental services, and other (semi-)government organizations. Focus on organizations wanting standardized, high-touch SaaS solutions for zaakgericht werken, digital forms, and citizen portals. Serves organizations of various sizes across the local government sector.

## Pricing

Not publicly disclosed. SaaS subscription pricing based on selected modules (Zaaksysteem, Formulieren, Mijn Portaal), number of users, and organization size. Contact Atabix for a custom quote.

## Key Features

- Modular architecture: Atabix Zaaksysteem, Atabix Formulieren (Forms), Atabix Mijn Portaal (My Portal)
- Standardized components that can be enabled or disabled as SaaS modules
- WCAG-compliant web forms with organizational branding
- Secure citizen/business communication portal
- Staff scheduling based on competencies and location
- Case creation, tracking, and handling in a unified interface
- Integration with national registrations and authentication (DigiD, eHerkenning)
- Configurable workflows and process templates
- Document management and archiving
- Microservices architecture for scalability
- Acquisition of Mozard brings additional VTH capabilities

## Strengths

- Modular SaaS approach allowing organizations to pick and choose components
- 25+ years of government domain expertise with 150+ employees dedicated to public sector
- Acquisition of Mozard creates a broad product portfolio covering zaakgericht werken, VTH, and DMS

## Weaknesses

- Proprietary and closed-source — vendor lock-in with no ability to inspect or modify the platform
- Ongoing integration of acquired products (Mozard, IenPM) may cause confusion and transition pains for customers
- No standards-based process modeling — workflows are proprietary rather than CMMN/BPMN

## Notes

Atabix is consolidating its position in the Dutch government software market through acquisitions. The combination of Atabix + Mozard creates a larger competitor with a broader product offering. However, the integration of multiple acquired products is a risk — merged product strategies can create confusion for customers. Procest competes with openness, standards compliance, and the modern Nextcloud ecosystem approach.
