# Capsule CRM — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://capsulecrm.com/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (cloud)
- **Summary:** Simple CRM with visual kanban pipeline and task tracking

## Business Model

SaaS subscription model with per-user pricing. Capsule is developed by Zestia Ltd, a UK-based company. Revenue from four paid subscription tiers plus a free plan for very small teams. Positioned as a simple, affordable CRM for small businesses that don't need enterprise complexity.

## Target Market

Small businesses and micro-teams (1-50 users) that want a simple, no-frills CRM. Popular with freelancers, consultants, small agencies, and professional services firms. Appeals to users who find HubSpot or Salesforce too complex. Strong in the UK and European markets.

## Pricing

- **Free:** Up to 2 users, 250 contacts
- **Starter:** $18/user/month
- **Growth:** $36/user/month
- **Advanced:** $54/user/month
- **Ultimate:** $72/user/month (includes dedicated customer success manager)
- 14-day free trial on all paid plans

## Key Features

- Contact and organization management with 360-degree view
- Visual kanban sales pipeline
- Task management with reminders
- Email integration (Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365)
- Activity tracking and communication history
- Custom fields and tags for segmentation
- Sales reporting and pipeline analytics
- Workflow automation (Growth plan and above)
- Project management (Advanced plan and above)
- 50+ third-party integrations (Xero, Mailchimp, Zapier)
- Mobile app (iOS and Android)

## Strengths

- Extremely simple and intuitive — fastest time-to-value of any CRM in this analysis
- Affordable pricing with a genuinely useful free tier for micro-teams
- Clean integration with common UK/European business tools (Xero, FreeAgent, Mailchimp)

## Weaknesses

- Limited features compared to full-featured CRMs — no marketing automation, no case management
- Proprietary cloud-only — no self-hosting, no data sovereignty, no code access
- No Nextcloud integration or Dutch government ecosystem support; limited RBAC

## Notes

Capsule CRM is the simplicity benchmark in this analysis. Its clean UX and straightforward pricing make it popular with small businesses that don't need advanced features. However, its feature set is limited compared to both Pipelinq and more complex CRMs. The 250-contact limit on the free plan is restrictive. For Dutch government use, the lack of self-hosting and Nextcloud integration are disqualifying factors.
