# ArkCase Solution Modules: Legal, Matter, and Complaint Management

**Source:** arkcase.com/solutions/

## Legal Case Management

### Tagline
"360 view of your case: AI-infused for great insight"

### Overview
ArkCase Legal Case Management is a FedRAMP Authorized solution for legal organizations, law firms, and government entities. It provides a cloud-based platform that streamlines case management workflows, centralizes case data, and ensures compliance with security protocols.

### Key Capabilities

#### 360-degree Case View
- Case, document, and records management
- Time and cost management
- People and organization management
- Notes and references

#### Document and Records Management
- 100+ document types in folders
- Online editing
- Email and calendar integration (O365/Exchange)
- Web document viewer with annotations and redactions
- Document tagging
- AI support for transcription and NLP
- Integrated records management for retention/disposition policies

#### Search, Reporting, and Analytics
- Metadata and full-text search
- Canned and ad-hoc reporting
- Scheduled tabular and graphical reports
- Analytics (predictive, dashboards, heatmaps)

#### Task and Workflow Automation
- Case and document approval workflows
- Automated and manual tasks
- Business rules engine

### AI/ML Capabilities
- Predictive intelligence for decision-making
- Automated PII redaction
- NLP for content capture
- Machine learning for compliance

### Compliance Standards
- FedRAMP Authorized
- HIPAA / HITECH
- Section 508
- VPAT available

### Customers
- Victoria Police Department
- Office of Personnel Management
- Top 100 US Law Firm (unnamed)

## Complaint Management

### Tagline
"User Friendly, Easily Accessible, and Customizable"

### Overview
ArkCase Complaint Management is a cloud-based platform that improves complaint handling efficiency with real-time access, customizable workflows, and seamless integration. It addresses challenges of outdated tools, lack of workload visibility, and difficulty tracking/collaborating on complaints.

### Key Capabilities

#### Multi-Channel Access
- Accessible from any device (including mobile)
- Customers, partners, and employees can reach out anytime
- Multiple intake channels

#### End-to-End Tracking
- Track complaints from submission to resolution
- Timely acknowledgment
- Process towards resolution
- Final beneficial outcome
- Multiple authorized users can track and collaborate

#### Trend Analysis
- Generate reports and audits
- Identify trends to prevent future complaints
- Easy-to-use reporting and auditing functions

#### Security and Confidentiality
- Restrict access when necessary
- Customizable user roles and access
- Field-level redaction on documents
- Annotation and redaction capabilities

### Open Architecture
- Easy integration with other applications
- Flexible response templates
- Customizable workflows
