# Folk — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.folk.app/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (cloud)
- **Summary:** Lightweight CRM with spreadsheet-like interface

## Business Model

SaaS subscription model with per-user pricing. Folk is a French startup (Y Combinator W22) that positions itself as a modern, AI-powered CRM for small teams. Revenue from three subscription tiers. The product emphasizes simplicity and AI features (contact enrichment, magic fields) as differentiators.

## Target Market

Small businesses, startups, and agencies (1-50 users) wanting a lightweight CRM that feels like a spreadsheet. Popular with teams that manage relationships across email, LinkedIn, and other channels. Appeals to users who find traditional CRMs too rigid and prefer a flexible, Airtable-like interface.

## Pricing

- **Standard:** $20/user/month ($19/user/month annual)
- **Premium:** $40/user/month ($39/user/month annual)
- **Custom:** From $80/user/month
- 14-day free trial on all plans
- No free tier
- Premium unlocks deals/pipeline, dashboards, email sequences, and API access

## Key Features

- Contact and company management with spreadsheet-like interface
- AI-powered contact enrichment (find emails, phone numbers, company data)
- Email integration with Gmail, Outlook, and WhatsApp sync
- Deal pipeline with kanban board (Premium plan)
- Email sequences for outreach campaigns
- Magic Fields (AI-generated fields from contact data)
- Group and segment contacts with custom views
- Chrome extension for importing contacts from LinkedIn, Gmail, etc.
- Calendar sync
- API access (Premium and above)
- Integrations with Zapier, Make, and native tools

## Strengths

- AI-powered contact enrichment — automatically finds emails, phone numbers, and company data
- Spreadsheet-like flexibility — users can customize views without rigid CRM structures
- Chrome extension for importing contacts directly from LinkedIn, Gmail, and websites

## Weaknesses

- Pipeline features locked behind Premium plan ($40/user/month) — expensive for core CRM functionality
- Proprietary cloud-only — no self-hosting, no data sovereignty, no open source
- No Nextcloud integration, no audit trail, limited RBAC; not suitable for enterprise or government use

## Notes

Folk represents the "modern lightweight CRM" trend alongside Attio. Its AI enrichment and Chrome extension are genuine differentiators for prospecting-focused teams. However, the pricing is steep for what you get — pipeline management requires the $40/user/month Premium plan. The spreadsheet-like interface is polarizing (some love it, others find it limiting). Not suitable for Dutch government use due to cloud-only model and lack of Nextcloud integration.
