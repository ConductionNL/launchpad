# Flowable Pricing and Support

Source: https://www.flowable.com/pricing

## Pricing Model

"Flexible platform. Flexible pricing." - User-based pricing with RCPI packages.

**RCPI** = Running Case & Process Instances

No public pricing is available. All commercial plans require contacting sales.
