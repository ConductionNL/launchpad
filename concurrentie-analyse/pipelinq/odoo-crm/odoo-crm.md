# Odoo CRM — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://www.odoo.com/app/crm
- **Open Source:** Yes (LGPL-3.0)
- **Self-Hosted:** Yes
- **Summary:** CRM module within the Odoo ERP ecosystem

## Codebase

- **Repository:** https://github.com/odoo/odoo

## Business Model

Open-core model. The Community Edition is free and open source (LGPL-3.0). Revenue comes from the Enterprise Edition (proprietary features like Studio, multi-company, external API), Odoo.sh hosting platform, and the Odoo Online SaaS offering. One app is free forever with unlimited users. The full suite requires a per-user subscription. Odoo also earns from its partner network of implementation consultants.

## Target Market

Small to large businesses across all industries. The modular ERP approach attracts companies that want CRM integrated with accounting, inventory, HR, and other business functions. Strong presence in European markets. Used by 12M+ users worldwide.

## Pricing

- **One App Free:** Free forever (pick one app, e.g., CRM, unlimited users)
- **Standard:** $31.10/user/month (all apps)
- **Custom:** $46.80/user/month (includes Odoo.sh, Studio, multi-company)
- Self-hosted Community Edition is free (limited features compared to Enterprise)

## Key Features

- Lead management with scoring and nurturing
- Visual kanban pipeline with drag-and-drop
- Activity scheduling and call management
- 360-degree contact view with communication history
- Email integration with templates
- Deduplication of leads and contacts
- VoIP integration for click-to-call
- Reporting and forecasting dashboards
- Address book with company/contact hierarchy
- Seamless integration with 80+ Odoo apps (Accounting, Inventory, HR, etc.)
- AI assistance and marketing automation (Enterprise)

## Strengths

- Part of a complete ERP ecosystem — CRM integrates natively with accounting, inventory, HR, and 80+ other modules
- One App Free tier genuinely useful — free CRM with unlimited users is compelling for startups
- Large global community (12M+ users), extensive documentation, and strong partner network

## Weaknesses

- Full functionality requires Enterprise Edition which is expensive at scale ($31+/user/month for all apps)
- Complexity of the full ERP can overwhelm teams that only need CRM
- No Nextcloud integration or Dutch government ecosystem support; Odoo pushes its own ecosystem

## Notes

Odoo CRM is the most broadly adopted competitor in this analysis. Its integration with the full Odoo ERP suite is its killer feature — no other open-source CRM offers this breadth. The One App Free tier is a strong acquisition strategy. However, for organizations that specifically need Nextcloud integration and Dutch government compliance (Common Ground, NL Design), Odoo is not a natural fit. Odoo promotes its own ecosystem rather than integrating with external platforms.
