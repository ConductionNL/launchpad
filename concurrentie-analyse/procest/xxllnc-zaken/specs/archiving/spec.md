# Archiving and Records Management (RMA)

competitor: xxllnc-zaken
analyzed_date: 2026-03-14
category: compliance
maturity: production
source: https://xxllnc.nl/applicaties/zaken

## Summary

xxllnc Zaken is a certified archive application (RMA — Records Management Application) that has been successfully tested against NEN-ISO 16175-1:2020 (formerly NEN-2082) standards. Archiving is integrated into the case management workflow, not a separate system.

## Capabilities

### NEN-ISO 16175-1:2020 Compliance
- Full certification against Dutch archiving standards
- Successor to NEN-2082 standard
- Covers both archiving and records management
- Ensures quality of records management within organizations

### Archive Integration
- Archive properties configurable per case type
- Archiving as part of the case lifecycle
- Case-level archive metadata management
- Supports the Archiefwet (Archive Law) requirements

### Common Ground Archive Component
- xxllnc Zaken adapted to function as Archive component
- Fits within Common Ground reference architecture
- Interoperable with other Common Ground components

### Records Management
- Retention schedule support (implied by NEN-ISO compliance)
- Document lifecycle management within cases
- Metadata standardization per NEN-ISO requirements
- Interchangeability of RM metadata between applications

## Strengths
- Formal NEN-ISO 16175-1:2020 certification — strong compliance story
- Archiving integrated into daily workflow (not a separate step)
- Common Ground compatible as archive component
- Proven with large-scale deployments (8M+ cases at Utrecht)
- Single system for case management and archiving

## Weaknesses
- Certification details not publicly available (test report, scope)
- No information about long-term preservation formats
- No mention of e-Depot integration
- Transfer to external archive (overbrenging) workflow unclear
- No information about destruction (vernietiging) procedures
- Audit trail capabilities not documented publicly

## Relevance to Procest

Procest should aim for:
1. **NEN-ISO 16175-1:2020 compliance** — match xxllnc's certification
2. **Integrated archiving** — archive properties as part of case workflow
3. **e-Depot integration** — connect to regional/national digital archives
4. **Destruction workflows** — automated retention and destruction processes
5. **Audit trail** — full traceability of case and document actions
6. **Open archive format** — export cases in standardized archive packages
7. **Archiefwet compliance** — ensure all legal requirements are met
