# Attio — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

- **Website:** https://attio.com/
- **Open Source:** No
- **Self-Hosted:** No (cloud)
- **Summary:** Modern flexible CRM on a relational data model

## Business Model

SaaS subscription model with per-user pricing and a free tier. Attio is a well-funded startup positioning itself as a next-generation CRM built on a flexible relational data model. Revenue from tiered subscriptions that unlock custom objects, advanced automations, and enterprise features. Growing rapidly in the startup and VC community.

## Target Market

Startups, tech companies, and venture capital/private equity firms. Appeals to teams that need a highly customizable CRM with a modern UI. Popular with organizations that outgrow simple CRMs but find Salesforce too complex. Strong in the startup ecosystem for managing investor relations, deal flow, and complex relationship networks.

## Pricing

- **Free:** Up to 3 users with basic CRM features, contact syncing, and data enrichment
- **Plus:** ~$36/user/month (removes seat limits, adds automations and advanced email)
- **Pro:** ~$86/user/month (custom objects, advanced automations, advanced permissions, reporting)
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing (SAML/SSO, advanced admin, flexible invoicing)

## Key Features

- Flexible relational data model with custom objects and attributes
- Real-time contact and company syncing from email and calendar
- Automatic data enrichment (company info, social profiles, demographics)
- Deal pipeline with kanban and list views
- Workflow automations with triggers and actions
- AI agents for automated tasks and insights
- Call intelligence and recording
- Advanced reporting and dashboards
- Email integration with sending and sharing
- Relationship mapping (stakeholder identification, company hierarchies)
- API for integrations
- SAML/SSO (Enterprise)

## Strengths

- Highly flexible data model with custom objects — can model any business process, not just standard CRM entities
- Automatic data enrichment and real-time contact syncing from email/calendar — minimal manual data entry
- Modern, polished UI that appeals to tech-savvy teams; AI agents and call intelligence are cutting-edge features

## Weaknesses

- Expensive — Pro plan at $86/user/month for custom objects makes it costly for growing teams
- Proprietary cloud-only — no self-hosting, no data sovereignty, no code access
- No Nextcloud integration or government ecosystem support; primarily targets the startup/VC market

## Notes

Attio represents the cutting edge of modern CRM design. Its flexible data model and automatic enrichment are genuinely innovative. However, the pricing is aggressive — custom objects (a core feature for many teams) require the $86/user/month Pro plan. Attio competes more with Salesforce than with lightweight CRMs. For Dutch government use, the cloud-only model and US-based hosting are disqualifying. Attio's data model flexibility is conceptually similar to what OpenRegister provides in the Pipelinq ecosystem.
